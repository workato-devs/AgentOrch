# Code of Conduct

## Our standards

AgentOrch contributors and maintainers are expected to communicate with
respect, assume good faith, welcome differing levels of experience, and keep
technical criticism focused on the work. Harassment, discrimination,
threats, deliberate disclosure of private information, and sustained
disruptive behavior are not acceptable in project spaces.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove contributions and may temporarily or
permanently restrict participation when behavior is inappropriate, unsafe, or
harmful to the community.

Report conduct concerns privately to the repository owner using the private
contact method on their GitHub profile. Do not include sensitive conduct
reports in a public issue. Reports will be reviewed as promptly and
confidentially as practical, and conflicts of interest should be disclosed so
another maintainer can handle the report.

This policy applies in repository discussions, issues, pull requests, review
comments, and project-related public events.
